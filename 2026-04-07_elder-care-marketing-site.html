The problem

Families are forced to coordinate deeply personal care across fragmented systems.

The hardest part often is not one emergency — it’s the ongoing fragmentation. Portals don’t talk to each other. Siblings carry different pieces of information. Small gaps become stress, confusion, and conflict.

✚

Medical chaos

Appointments, medications, specialists, discharge paperwork, provider messages — critical context lives across too many places.

◔

Financial complexity

Bills, insurance claims, spending, savings, power of attorney, and estate visibility become emotionally loaded fast.

⌂

Physical care coordination

Home support, transportation, safety checks, maintenance, and the question of aging in place versus a facility all need oversight.

Three pillars of care

One platform built around the realities families actually face.

The product is structured around the three domains that define long-term elder care: medical, physical, and financial.

⚕

Medical

Appointments, medications, clinical updates, provider communication, and care plan continuity.

❤

Physical

In-home support, transportation, safety, household help, and decisions about aging in place.

◫

Financial

Expenses, insurance, savings visibility, benefit coordination, and clearer family transparency.
