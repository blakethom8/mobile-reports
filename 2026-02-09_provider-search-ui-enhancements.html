Major UI/UX improvements focused on consistency across views, a redesigned status system, and improved information hierarchy. The application now defaults to Map view with automatic provider selection for immediate context.

Status System Overhaul

Complete redesign of the provider status system with professional naming and distinct visual colors.

Status Icon Color Use Case
● Hard Lead ● Green (emerald) High-priority prospect, ready for outreach
◐ Soft Lead ◐ Blue Potential prospect, needs more research
◆ Existing ◆ Purple Already in network/relationship exists
○ Non-Target ○ Orange Not a fit for current campaign
? Does Not Exist ? Dark Gray Business closed, moved, or data incorrect
