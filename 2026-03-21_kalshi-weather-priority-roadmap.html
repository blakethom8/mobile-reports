🛣️ Kalshi Weather Priority Roadmap

Prepared by Chief | March 21, 2026

What we should build now, what comes next, and what should wait until the foundation is honest

Roadmap principle: build the truth and replay layers before spending too much energy on clever gating, calibration, and configuration. Otherwise we risk polishing abstractions on top of only-partly-real data.

Priority Order

PriorityWorkstreamStatusWhy this order makes sense
1Settlement / truth adapterNOWThe forecast side is increasingly real; the truth side still lags. We should not calibrate or tune against weak labels.
2Source-backed decision replayNOWBefore we build more reporting features, the system should be able to replay a full candidate bet from source-backed data and explain itself.
3Strategy config layerNEXTVery valuable, but much better once truth + replay are solid.
4Decision gate reportNEXTBest built as a thin presentation layer on top of a real decision pipeline.
5Confidence tracking / city calibrationNEXTHigh value, but depends on good labels, enough source-backed rows, and a stable replay loop.
6Paper trading / richer evaluatorLATERShould follow once decision replay and review surfaces are stronger.

Current Sprint Focus

Foundation Sprint

  1. Implement a real-ish settlement source adapter and fixtures
  2. Wire a source-backed decision replay path
  3. Tighten the test environment around those two layers

Why not jump straight to strategy config?

Because configuration is only as honest as the system underneath it. We want config parameters to control a real pipeline, not a half-synthetic one.

Healthy sequencing: source-backed truth → source-backed replay → strategy config → gate report → calibration tracking.

Planned Feature Backlog

  • strategy config layer
  • decision gate report
  • city-specific calibration dashboard / report
  • richer abstention logging
  • bet postmortem / lessons layer
  • paper trading with execution realism
