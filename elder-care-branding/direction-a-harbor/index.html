You're not imagining it

Caring for a parent has quietly become a second job.

Usually one sibling carries it. The others want to help, but nobody has the full picture. It isn't anyone's fault. The systems were never built for families.

The medical maze

Twelve patient portals. Four doctors who don't talk to each other. A discharge summary nobody explained. You've become the messenger between systems that should already be connected.

The financial fog

Bills in three places, a subscription no one remembers starting, an insurance letter that reads like a riddle. You worry about what you can't see — and about asking to see it.

The daily juggle

Rides to appointments. Groceries and home repairs. Who's calling Mom this week? The group text keeps everyone talking — and no one aligned.

If any of this feels familiar, you're in the right place. And you're not alone.

Questions families ask

The things you're wondering. Answered plainly.

Does my parent have to agree to this?+

Yes — and we'd have it no other way. Every connection to your parent's health or financial information starts with their explicit consent, gathered by a real person, in plain language. Your parent chooses what's shared, with whom, and can change their mind at any time. If your parent has a legal decision-maker (like a power of attorney), we work within that framework and document it carefully.

Who in my family can see what?+

That's up to your family. Harbor uses roles and permissions built for real family dynamics: one sibling might see everything, another might see health updates but not bank balances. Your parent — and your family — set those boundaries during onboarding, and every access is recorded in an audit trail everyone can review. Nobody, including us, operates out of sight.

How is our information kept private and secure?+

Harbor is HIPAA compliant and protects your data with 256-bit encryption, the same standard your bank uses. We never sell data — not to advertisers, not to insurers, not to anyone. Our business is your subscription, full stop. Connections to banks and health portals use secure, read-only channels wherever possible, and you can disconnect any account instantly.

What does the assistant actually do — and what doesn't it do?+

It does the legwork: logging into portals so you don't have to, summarizing visits and statements in plain language, tracking follow-ups, flagging changes like a new medication or an unusual charge. It does not make medical or financial decisions, and it doesn't act on anything significant without your family's say-so. When something needs judgment, it goes to your family — and to your Family Success Representative if you want a human in the loop.

Is there a real person we can talk to?+

Always. Every family is matched with a dedicated Family Success Representative — one person, by name, who handles your onboarding, checks in every quarter, and picks up when things get hard. They're employees, not a call center, and they stay with your family over time.

What if we start and it isn't right for us?+

Then you leave — gently and completely. There are no contracts and no cancellation fees. If you go, we help you export your family's records and we delete your data on your timeline. We'd rather earn your trust every month than lock you into anything.
