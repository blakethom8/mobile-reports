Direction A · Harbor · Creative Review

Advertising Suite — "The calm hand on the shoulder"

Every unit below is built to one rule: meet a stressed adult child at a hard moment without exploiting it. No countdowns, no fear, no "AI" in a headline. Recognition first, reassurance second, one gentle next step.

Units: 3 display · 3 social · 2 email · 1 OOH Voice: reassuring, plain-spoken, short sentences Status: for internal review

A · Display banners — true sizes

Retargeting and contextual placements (caregiving content, health publishers, local news). Display carries the brand promise, not a pitch — these are often seen at low attention, so one serif line does the work.

Paid social intercepts people mid-scroll during the exact life moments when eldercare becomes real. Each card leads with recognition — the reader's own week, described accurately and kindly — before Harbor says a word about itself.

C · Email — lifecycle templates (600px)

Email is where Harbor's human layer becomes literal: mail comes from a named Family Success Representative, not "The Harbor Team." One idea per email, one action, always an easy exit.
