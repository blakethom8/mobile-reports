II · Print

Long copy for readers who still read

Print reaches Meridian's two channels where they are already comfortable: wealth-management publications for the affluent family, and health-plan member publications for the co-branded benefit. Both executions honor the oldest rule of luxury advertising — the confident brand explains itself fully, once, and does not repeat itself.

A Private Care Office for Your Parents

He spent fifty years keeping his affairs in order. That order is now yours to keep.

There is a morning most families remember. The one where a discharge summary, a bank statement, and a question about the house all arrive in the same week, and it becomes quietly clear that someone must now hold the whole picture of a parent's life — and that the someone is you.

Most families improvise from there: portals and passwords, a group text with a hierarchy nobody agreed to, one sibling carrying too much while the others learn things late. The affairs of a careful lifetime deserve better than improvisation.

Meridian is a private care office for your parents. A dedicated Care Director — a named senior specialist, not a call center — stewards their medical, household, and financial affairs, supported by intelligent systems that watch every connected record so no change goes unnoticed and no follow-up goes unkept.

Your family sees the same composed picture, each member exactly what their role permits. Your parents' consent governs all of it; every access is written to a permanent audit record. Each quarter, the family convenes with its Care Director and leaves with the same understanding — the whole estate of care, reviewed like the serious matter it is.

It is, we admit, an old idea: the family office, applied to the one asset class that matters most. We simply believe it should not be rare. Engagements from $79 a month; income-scaled pricing available, and no one will ever know the difference.

IV · Email

Correspondence, not campaigns

Meridian email is written as letters from a person with a name, set at a single 600-pixel column. The two templates below are the highest-stakes messages the brand will ever send: the first impression after an advisor referral, and the first morning of a new engagement.
