The Member Experience

How the benefit works for your members

Meridian is offered as a supplemental or member-services benefit for targeted cohorts — recent discharge, multiple chronic conditions, cognitive impairment, polypharmacy, evident caregiver strain. The experience is designed so the member and family feel privately attended, not case-managed.

I.

Cohort identification

You define eligibility with your actuarial and care management teams; we support targeting logic drawn from discharge events, condition flags, and caregiver-strain indicators.

II.

A co-branded invitation

Members receive a dignified, plan-branded introduction — a benefit conferred, not a program assigned. Families enroll by phone or online, at no cost or a plan-set contribution.

III.

Private onboarding

A Meridian Care Director onboards the family — parent first, consent documented, roles and permissions established. Your member is treated as our client, because they are.

IV.

Coordination between visits

Discharge plans tracked to completion. Medication changes surfaced same-day. Follow-ups kept. Escalations routed to your care management team through agreed channels.

V.

Reporting you can use

Engagement, transitions completed, follow-up adherence, and utilization signals — cohort-level and privacy-preserving, aligned to your quality and Stars-experience priorities.

The Plan Proposition

What Meridian does for the plan

UtilizationFewer avoidable events

Most preventable ED visits and readmissions in complex elders are operational failures — medication confusion, missed follow-up, transportation gaps, an unaligned family. These are precisely the failures a coordinated household stops. The ROI case concentrates in high-risk cohorts, where preventing a small fraction of events covers the benefit for the entire population.

TransitionsDischarges that hold

Meridian receives the discharge summary into a family that is organized to execute it: tasks assigned, medications reconciled against the pharmacy record, follow-ups scheduled and kept, symptoms watched by people who know what to watch for — with a Care Director accountable for the handoff landing.

ExperienceSatisfaction that reaches the caregiver

The adult daughter coordinating a member's care influences disenrollment decisions, complaint volume, and the member's experience of your plan — and no survey reaches her. Meridian does. Families with a functioning coordination layer generate fewer service escalations and remember which plan gave them one.

StructurePEPM, targeted, and accountable

Per-eligible-per-month pricing on the cohort you define, with tiered intensity — from Connected-level visibility to fully Managed coordination for your most complex households. Pilot structures with agreed measurement plans are our preferred starting point; we would rather earn the expansion.

BrandCo-branded to your standard

Meridian was designed to sit inside a member benefits packet without apology — a restrained, editorial identity that carries your mark alongside ours. Member-facing materials, enrollment experiences, and correspondence are co-branded and subject to your review.

ComplianceHIPAA posture, ready for your diligence

Business associate agreement executed as a matter of course. Encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, complete audit trails of human and machine activity, documented consent architecture, breach protocols, and recurring independent security assessment. Our security and compliance documentation is prepared for your review before the first workshop.

You see the family chaos first. Now you have somewhere worthy to send it.

Elder law attorneys, wealth managers, geriatric care managers, and concierge physicians sit closest to families at the moment coordination begins to fail — and have long lacked a referral that reflects their own standard of practice. Concierge MedicineThe household your care plan assumes

Your medicine presumes an organized family: medications taken as adjusted, follow-ups kept, changes reported. Meridian makes that presumption true, and — with consent — gives your practice a reliable channel to the family members who actually execute the plan, without another portal for anyone.

The referral, conducted to your standard

  1. White-glove intake, within one business day. Every referred family is received by a senior Care Director — never a queue — who will have reviewed whatever context you choose to share. You are told, promptly, that the family is in good hands.
  2. Co-stewardship, within your mandate. We coordinate around your role rather than across it. Your authority with the family is respected in our permissions model literally: advisors hold defined roles with defined visibility, granted by the family.
  3. Reporting back, with the family's consent. Where the family consents in writing, your office receives the reporting relevant to your work — quarterly or at the cadence you prefer. Where they do not, we tell you so plainly. Their consent governs; you would want it no other way.
  4. No referral fees, in either direction. We do not pay for introductions and do not accept payment for the providers we recommend to families. Your referral remains what it should be: professional judgment, cleanly given. For fiduciaries, we will put this in writing.
