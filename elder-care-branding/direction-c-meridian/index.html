Your Care Director

A named specialist. Not a queue, not a chatbot, not a call center.

Every Meridian family is assigned a Care Director — a senior specialist who leads your onboarding, learns your family's particulars, and remains your single point of contact for as long as the engagement holds. You will know their name. They will know your parents'.

Care Directors are chosen for judgment and discretion as much as expertise. They have coordinated care through hospital discharges at midnight and estate questions no one wanted to raise at dinner. They know when to act, when to ask, and when to simply keep watch.

Behind each Care Director stands Meridian's intelligence layer — purpose-built systems that retrieve, reconcile, and monitor across every connected institution. The systems provide the vigilance; your Care Director provides the judgment. You are never asked to trust a machine with a decision that belongs to a person.

Trust & Governance

The fine print, kept in plain view.

Families grant Meridian access to the most sensitive information they hold. We regard the terms of that access as a first-order feature of the service — written plainly, enforced technically, and inspectable at any hour.

Article IConsent precedes access

Nothing is connected, viewed, or shared without documented consent — beginning with your parents. Where capacity is a question, we work within the legal instruments your family and its advisors have established, and we help you put them in place if they are missing. Consent can be narrowed or withdrawn at any time, and the change takes effect immediately.

Article IIAuthority is defined, not assumed

Each family member holds an explicit role with explicit permissions. A daughter with power of attorney and a son who should see medical updates but not bank balances are different roles, and the system treats them differently. Sensitive actions require approval from those with the standing to give it.

Article IIIEvery action is recorded

Every view, change, and instruction — human or machine — is written to a permanent audit record that authorized family members may inspect at any time. In a family, an unimpeachable record is not bureaucracy. It is how trust survives stress.

Article IVHealth information, handled to the standard

Meridian's handling of health information is built to HIPAA's requirements: minimum-necessary access, business associate agreements with connected parties, workforce training, and breach protocols we hope never to use and rehearse anyway.

Article VEncryption without exception

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest to the standards used by the banks we connect to. Credentials are never stored in plain text, access is least-privilege by default, and our security posture is independently assessed on a recurring schedule.

Most firms bury these terms in an appendix. We consider them the product.

From Our Families · A Case Study

The Hartwells, and the winter that did not become a crisis.

When Margaret Hartwell's husband died, her three children discovered what he had been quietly managing: eleven accounts at four institutions, a cardiologist and a rheumatologist who had never spoken, and a house in Charleston with its own weather of contractors and deliveries. Anne was in Boston, David in Denver, Claire forty minutes away and suddenly, by geography alone, the appointed one.

Within a year the family had become what many families become — a group text with a hierarchy no one had agreed to. Claire knew everything and resented it. David knew nothing and resented that. Anne learned of her mother's emergency room visit two days afterward, from a forwarded voicemail.

Their mother's estate attorney suggested Meridian. What the Hartwells remember about onboarding is that the first substantive meeting was with Margaret alone. Her Care Director asked what she wanted her children to see, what she preferred to keep private, and how she wished to be asked. "It was the first time in that whole year," Margaret said later, "that someone started with me."

"It was the first time in that whole year that someone started with me."

The months since have been unremarkable, which is the point. A medication change made in a Tuesday appointment appeared in the family brief that afternoon, with a note from the Care Director about what it meant and what it did not. When the pharmacy's records disagreed with the discharge summary, Meridian caught the discrepancy before the first refill. When an unfamiliar recurring charge surfaced against Margaret's checking account, it was flagged, investigated, and resolved before the second one posted.

In January, Margaret fell on the porch stairs — the kind of event that once would have detonated across three time zones. Instead: the emergency contacts worked, the hospital had her current medication list within the hour, and by evening all three children had read the same composed summary and knew who was doing what. She was home in two days. The quarterly review that followed was mostly about the garden.

Claire still lives forty minutes away. She is no longer the only one who knows things. "I got to go back to being her daughter," she says. "That was what we were paying for, it turns out."

Questions, Answered Plainly

What families ask us

Is Meridian a home-care agency or a medical provider?+

No. Meridian is the coordination layer above them — a private care office. We do not employ aides or practice medicine; we make certain that the physicians, agencies, facilities, and institutions already in your parents' lives operate as one coherent whole, and that your family always holds the full picture. Where a new provider is needed, we help you find and vet one, and we accept no commissions from anyone we recommend.

How do my parents remain in control?+

Structurally. The engagement begins with your parents, not around them: their consent governs what is connected and who sees it, and that consent is theirs to narrow or withdraw at any time. Many of our parents are active participants — some are the ones who engaged us. Where cognitive decline is part of the picture, we work within the legal authorities your family has established, and with the same regard we would want shown to our own parents. A parent is never a project at Meridian. This is the firmest commitment we make.

Who in the family sees what?+

Exactly what their role permits, and nothing more. Roles and permissions are established at onboarding and adjustable as circumstances change. One sibling may hold financial authority while another sees medical updates only. Every access is written to an audit record the family can inspect — which, in our experience, ends more arguments than it starts.

What do the intelligent systems actually do?+

The legwork. They retrieve and reconcile records across patient portals, financial institutions, pharmacies, and insurers; they detect changes and anomalies; they draft summaries, correspondence, and next steps for your Care Director's review. They do not make decisions about your parents' care. Judgment remains with people — your family first, your Care Director in support.

Can our attorney, wealth manager, or care manager be involved?+

Yes, and often to great effect. With the family's written consent, Meridian can furnish your advisors with the reporting relevant to their work — an estate attorney kept current on documents, a wealth manager on care expenditures. Many families come to us through these advisors. We pay no referral fees and accept none; the relationship stays clean.

What happens in an emergency?+

The preparation happens long before. Current medication lists, conditions, contacts, and directives are maintained and shareable with treating clinicians in moments. When an event occurs, your Care Director manages the coordination — hospital communication, family briefing, discharge planning — so the family can be present as a family. Managed-level engagements include priority after-hours response.

How is our information protected?+

Encryption in transit and at rest to bank standards; HIPAA-aligned handling of health information; least-privilege access; recurring independent security assessment; and a complete audit record of every access, human or machine. The full security summary is available in writing before you connect anything, and our team will walk your family — or your advisors — through it.

Can we change engagement levels, or conclude the engagement?+

At any time, in either direction, without penalty. Families often begin at Connected and deepen as needs grow. Should you conclude the engagement, your records are exported to you in full and our copies are destroyed on a documented schedule. What Meridian holds, it holds in trust — never as leverage.
