Disconnect Banner Health MyChart?

Here's exactly what changes — nothing more, nothing less.

  • We'll stop seeing new visit notes and lab results
  • Medication list stops updating — last saved copy stays

HIPAA & your rights

Harbor stores health information under HIPAA-aligned safeguards. You can see it, correct it, export it, and delete it. Full notice: harbor.care/privacy

Close your family workspace

If Harbor isn't right for your family, leaving is simple and honest:

  • 30-day recovery window — change your mind, everything comes back
  • Connected accounts are disconnected immediately
  • We'll offer a full export first, so nothing is lost
  • Elena is notified — it's her information too

If Elena passes away

When a family tells us their parent has died, Harbor shifts to Memorial mode. Billing stops that day — no final invoice. Her records stay available to the family for two years for estate and closure needs, then are gently archived unless you choose otherwise.

No upsells, no automated cheerfulness — and Grace personally reaches out, once, to say goodbye and help with practical next steps.

"Your data belongs to your family. We are its caretakers, never its owners."

Bank-level encryption · HIPAA-aligned · Every access is logged

Design intent. End-of-life is a product state, not an edge case — it's designed, written, and reviewed like any other flow. Billing stops the same day because charging a grieving family even one extra day is a brand-ending act. The export and delete cards use the same honest-consequences pattern as Frame 2: the exits are as dignified as the entrances.
