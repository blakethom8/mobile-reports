Before anything is shared: we ask Elena.

Harbor never opens a window into someone's life without their okay. Choose how Elena would like to be asked.

Elena can't take part in this decision right now? I have power of attorney / guardianship — verify documents instead

Harbor verifies POA and guardianship documents with a human review, usually within two business days. We'll still keep Elena's view open to her if her situation changes.

What Elena will be asked

  • Who wants to share with her: Marisol, Daniel, and Ana.
  • What kinds of information: appointments, medications, and — only if she chooses — bills and health records.
  • That she can say no to any part — yes to appointments, no to finances is common, and fine.
  • That she can change her mind anytime, and Harbor will honor it the same day.
