Brand directions

Each direction is a complete, self-consistent hypothesis: a full marketing site plus an advertising suite (display, social, email, and channel-specific units) with strategic annotations.

Direction A

Harbor

"The calm hand on the shoulder"

Warm, dignified, human-first. Built around the exhausted primary coordinator. Headspace meets One Medical — relief over productivity, safety over hype.

Marketing site Ad suite
Direction B

Trellis

"The family's chief of staff"

Modern, confident, quietly cool. Product-forward: from group-text chaos to one command center. The structure that supports the family's growth.

Marketing site Ad suite
Direction C

Meridian

"Your family's private care office"

Quiet luxury and permanence — a family-office feel built for the affluent ICP and the advisor / health-plan channel. Discretion. Coordination. Continuity.
