One workspace, whole picture

Care isn't one job. It's three, running at once.

Most tools solve one slice — a calendar here, a document vault there. Trellis runs medical, daily, and financial care in a single system, because that's how the work actually arrives.

Medical

Every portal, provider, and prescription — synced, summarized, and watched for gaps.

  • Appointments and follow-ups on one shared timeline
  • Discharge summaries translated into plain-English next steps
  • Medication list with refill status and change alerts
  • Health-portal sync — one connection, permissioned for the family

Daily life

The unglamorous logistics that keep a household running — with named owners, not vague hopes.

  • Home care and aide schedules, visible to every sibling
  • Rides to appointments assigned before they're urgent
  • Home maintenance, groceries, and errands as real tasks

Financial

Balances, bills, and paperwork in the open — so money stops being the thing nobody talks about.

  • Connected accounts with read-only, role-based visibility

  • Unusual-spend flags, from duplicate charges to scam patterns
  • Insurance claims, POA, and estate docs where the family can find them
Agents on staff

The legwork gets done. You get the signal.

Behind your family's workspace, Trellis agents log into the systems you already use — banks, health portals, facilities, insurers — retrieve what changed, cross-reference it, and surface only what matters. Not a chatbot with opinions. A staff that does the reading.

  • Retrieval, not reminders. Agents pull the discharge summary, the claim status, the statement — you don't chase them.
  • Synthesis across domains. A pharmacy trend plus a utility spike is a health signal. Only a system that sees both can connect them.
  • Every signal cites its sources. Tap any alert to see exactly which record, statement, or portal it came from. Trust, but verify — we built the verify button.
  • Decisions stay with your family. Agents tee up the next step and the context. People choose. Always.

Humans when it matters

Software runs the system. A person has your back.

Every Trellis family gets a Family Success Representative — a real, named human who sets you up, knows your situation, and picks up when things get complicated. Because nobody should navigate a discharge, a diagnosis, or a difficult sibling conversation with a help-center article.

  1. White-glove onboarding

    Your FSR walks the whole family through setup — accounts, portals, roles, consent — in one guided session. Mom included, at her pace.

  2. Quarterly reviews

    Every few months, a standing check-in: what's changed, what's coming, what the system should watch next. Care evolves. So does your setup.

  3. Escalation, on demand

    Insurance dispute? Facility transition? A form nobody can decode? Your FSR takes it from "someone should handle this" to handled.

Questions, answered straight

The things families actually ask us.

Is this safe? You're asking for bank and medical access.

We are, and we treat that like the responsibility it is. Trellis is HIPAA compliant, uses bank-level encryption in transit and at rest, and connects to financial accounts through read-only credentials — we can see, never move, money. Every access by every person (and every agent) is logged in an audit trail your whole family can review. We'll walk you through our full security posture during onboarding, in plain English.

What do the AI agents actually do?

Legwork. They log into the portals, banks, and facility systems your family connects, retrieve what changed, and synthesize it — "two follow-ups missed since discharge," "medication list changed Tuesday," "utility bill up 40% while pharmacy spend dropped." Every signal cites its sources so you can verify it in one tap. What they don't do: make decisions, move money, or talk to your mother's doctor without a human choosing to send it.

Does my mom have to use the app?

No — but she's welcome in it, and we hope she joins. The parent is a member of their own workspace, not a subject of it. She can see what's shared about her, and connecting her accounts starts with her consent, guided gently by your FSR. Many parents use Trellis just to see the week ahead and who's visiting. Some run the whole thing themselves. Her call.

What if my siblings won't sign up?

Start anyway. One organized sibling with Trellis beats four disorganized ones without it — and in our experience, the weekly family brief does the recruiting for you. When a brother in another city gets one clear summary instead of forty texts, he tends to log in. Roles mean skeptical siblings can start "informed-only" and lean in at their own pace.

Who are the Family Success Representatives?

Real employees — not a call center, not a chatbot with a human name. Your family is assigned one named FSR who runs your onboarding, holds your quarterly reviews, and takes your escalations. They're trained in care navigation, family dynamics, and elder consent practices. When you call, Dana knows who Ruth is.

Can we cancel? What happens to our data?

Cancel anytime, no phone-tree obstacle course. You get a complete export of your family's records, timeline, and documents in standard formats, and we delete our copies on your schedule. Data is your family's, full stop — it's not a hostage and it's not a product.

We can't afford $149 a month right now.

Tell us. Income-scaled pricing and full scholarships exist on every tier because our mission is one million families, not one million invoices. The application takes about five minutes and doesn't change what you get — same product, same FSR, same everything.
