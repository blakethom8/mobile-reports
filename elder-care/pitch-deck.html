Families Are Drowning in Care Coordination

Medical Care

Doctor appointments, medications, provider portals, insurance claims, and specialist referrals spread across dozens of systems.

Physical Care

In-home support scheduling, transportation coordination, daily living assistance, and monitoring safety across multiple caregivers.

Financial Care

Bank accounts, investment assets, insurance policies, estate planning, bills, and benefits coordination across institutions.

"There's a lot of coordination and a lot of different portals... When there's a lack of retrieval of information, there becomes a distrust amongst siblings."

53M+ Americans are caregivers, averaging 24 hours/week on care tasks

Onboarding Is Our Moat — And Our Biggest Lever

The Moat

Every onboarding is a human-to-human experience. This builds the trust that lets us connect bank accounts, medical records, and care systems. No competitor can replicate this with software alone.

The Lever

At 6 hours per family, onboarding FSRs are 45–60% of our total FSR labor. AI-guided onboarding could cut this to 3 hours — halving onboarding headcount and saving $625K/year at scale.

"Almost every onboarding process should be a human-to-human experience. That is the service that we offer that is a differentiator."
