# Three-Year Organic Growth Strategy

## Executive Summary

The first 36 months are about building a **sustainable, profitable elder care platform** through organic growth — proving the model works before pursuing health system partnerships. By Year 3, we target **~4,500 active families** generating **$13.3M ARR** at strong margins, funded by approximately **$1.25M in startup capital**.

This is not a race to scale. It's a deliberate build-and-prove strategy that positions us to accelerate from strength in Years 4-5.

## Growth Phases

**Goal:** Product-market fit with first ~800 families

| Metric | Target |

**Key milestones:**
- First 100 families onboarded

- Unit economics validated at small scale

### Phase 2: Early Growth (Months 13-24)
**Goal:** Double the growth rate, hit profitability

| Metric | Target |

**What's happening:**

**Key milestones:**
- Monthly profitability achieved
- Cumulative cash deficit begins recovering

- Churn rate validated at meaningful scale (2,000+ families)

**Goal:** Proven, profitable business at ~4,500 families

**Key milestones:**

## The Onboarding Reality

Onboarding dominates FSR labor throughout the 3-year plan. At 6 hours per family onboarding:

| Phase | New/Month | Onboarding FSRs | Service FSRs | Total FSRs | % in Onboarding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build & Launch | 75 | 3 | 2 | 5-6 | ~60% |
| Early Growth | 150 | 6 | 6 | 12 | 50% |
| Scale | 250 | 10 | 12 | 22 | 45% |

Onboarding FSR demand is driven purely by growth rate (new families / 26.7 capacity per FSR). Service FSR demand is driven by total active families (active / 400 panel size). Early on, onboarding dominates because the active family base is still small relative to the intake volume.

**The single highest-leverage improvement** is reducing onboarding hours per family. If AI-guided onboarding (the Clicky concept) can reduce onboarding from 6 hours to 3 hours, the 10 onboarding FSRs in Phase 3 become 5 — saving $625K/year in salary costs alone.

## Churn Model

We model **2% monthly churn** (~21.5% annual), which accounts for:

- **1.67% structural churn** — the natural end of a ~5-year elder care journey (end-of-life, transition to facility care, family situation resolved). This is inherent to the category and cannot be reduced.
- **0.33% voluntary churn** — families who leave for preventable reasons (dissatisfaction, affordability, switching providers). This is already very low.

At scale, churn creates significant replacement demand:

| Active Families | Monthly Replacement Needed |
|---|---|
| 2,000 | 40 families/month |
| 4,500 | 90 families/month |
| 10,000 | 200 families/month |

By Year 3 with 4,500 families, ~90 of our 250 monthly onboardings (36%) are replacing churned families. This replacement demand is permanent and grows with scale — it's the primary reason cost-effective acquisition channels (like health plan partnerships) become important beyond Year 3.

## Beyond Year 3: The Partnership Opportunity

The 3-year organic plan deliberately stops short of the full market opportunity. At Year 3, we have:

- A proven, profitable platform managing 4,500 families
- Validated unit economics (10.4x LTV:CAC, $206 contribution margin)
- A trained FSR workforce and hiring pipeline
- Data and case studies from 3 years of operations

This positions us to pursue **health system partnerships** in Years 4-5:

| Scenario | Year 5 Families | Year 5 ARR | Total FSRs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic only (continue 250/mo) | ~6,500 | $19M | ~30 |
| One health plan partner (+200/mo) | ~10,000 | $30M | ~45 |
| Multiple partners (+400/mo) | ~15,000 | $45M | ~65 |

A single health plan partnership could accelerate us from 4,500 to 10,000 families within 18-24 months. The key insight: **we don't need partnerships to survive — we choose to pursue them from profitability.**

## Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $249/family | Based on competitive analysis and willingness-to-pay research |
| Onboarding fee | $500 one-time | Covers setup labor costs |
| Onboarding hours | 6 hrs/family | Conservative; AI-guided onboarding could reduce to 3 hrs |
| Monthly churn | 2% | 1.67% structural (5-year lifecycle) + 0.33% voluntary |
| FSR salary | $125K fully loaded | Competitive for care coordination roles |
| Families per service FSR | 400 | Panel size target for ongoing management |
| AI/cloud cost | $5/family/month | Technology infrastructure per subscriber |
| Paid acquisition cost | $600/family | Marketing and sales cost per new family |
